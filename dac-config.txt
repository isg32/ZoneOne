The CJMCU-1334 UDA1334A I2S DAC Audio Stereo Decoder Module is a high-quality digital-to-analog converter (DAC) designed to decode I2S audio signals into stereo analog audio output. It uses the UDA1334A IC to deliver high-fidelity audio conversion for audio systems, sound projects, and embedded applications. Ideal for audio players, sound systems, and embedded projects that require stereo audio decoding from digital I2S audio sources.
Features:

    IC Used: UDA1334A I2S audio DAC.
    Audio Output: Provides stereo analog output (L/R channels).
    Input Interface: Supports I2S digital audio input.
    Audio Quality: High-quality audio decoding for clear sound reproduction.
    Power Supply: Works with 5V supply voltage.
    Compact Module: Small, easy-to-integrate form factor for embedded projects.
    Applications: Ideal for audio players, sound systems, and embedded devices requiring I2S to stereo DAC conversion.
